About me

I’m Filippa Persson, a 23 year old Interaction Design student at Stockholm University. Born in Gothenburg, now based in Stockholm.

I’ve always been drawn to the creative side of things and today that passion has found its place in my studies and work. I’m particularly interested in colour, typography, form, imagery and composition, and how these elements come together to create a strong visual identity. I love creating experiences that not only work well, but also feel cohesive, considered and exciting.

Through my studies, I have gained experience working across the entire design process, from exploring problems and developing concepts to creating prototypes and user-centered designs. While I’m particularly drawn to UI and visual design, I believe that great design is more than just how something looks. Without a thoughtful user experience, even strong visual design can lose its purpose and impact. I’m a creative person, a problem solver and a bit of a doer. I enjoy working with others, throwing around ideas and bringing them to life, but I’m also happy taking the lead and getting things done. I like structure, I like creating, and I love seeing an idea turn into something real.

Beyond digital design, you’ll find me taking photos, editing videos or exploring graphic design and other creative projects for fun.

Let’s turn ideas into something real!

I would describe myself as a social, happy and positive person who loves trying new things. This can mean anything from trying a new sport or taking on a challenge to exploring new places or testing a new recipe. Whatever I do, I always want to learn, develop and become better at what I do.

I have been active since childhood and have tried many different sports, but figure skating and cheerleading have always been closest to my heart. Both combine show, eye-catching competition outfits, and routines with many different elements. You train hard for a long time just to be in the spotlight for 2–3 minutes. Cheerleading, in particular, taught me the importance of teamwork, trust and understanding that everyone matters, no matter their role.

Skiing has been part of my life since I was 18 months old and I absolutely love it. What started as a hobby has also become a job, as I had the opportunity to work with skiing the winter of 2025. To me, there is something special about working with something you truly enjoy and care about! I also work as a Brand Ambassador for New Balance, where I represent the brand in stores, at community events and through running activities. So yes, I run too! At my part-time job at Thule, I enjoy creating great customer experiences and working with quality and the people the brand is made for.

Besides sports and staying active, I love capturing moments, whether with my phone or a proper camera. My friends could tell you how often I take photos and videos! I am also interested in fashion and interior design, nothing beats the feeling of putting together a really nice outfit!
